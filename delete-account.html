Delete your account

Bowl: Restaurant Costs & P&L · made by Eli's Group LLC

In the app

Open Bowl and go to Settings → Account → Request account deletion. Only the restaurant's owner can make this request in the app.

By email

Email info@get-bowl.com from the email address you sign in with, and ask us to delete your account. Requests are completed within 30 days.

What happens

  • Your account becomes read-only right away. You can still view your data, but nothing can be added or changed.
  • We notify the Bowl team, and we'll reach out within 2 business days.
  • 30 days after your request, your account is deleted automatically. This removes your restaurant and all of its data — invoices and their images, ingredients, prices, recipes, costs, sales, labor and team data, settings and notifications — and your sign-in account.
  • You can cancel the request at any time during those 30 days, in the same place in the app. Cancelling restores normal use, with nothing lost.

What we keep

Only a minimal record that a deletion happened (account identifiers and timestamps, no restaurant content), to show that we honored the request, and records the law requires us to keep, such as billing records. Copies in our backups are overwritten in the normal course of backup rotation. See our Privacy Policy.
