Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Bowl is made by Elis Group LLC. We want everyone who runs or works in a restaurant to be able to use our website and app, including people who use assistive technology.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, on get-bowl.com and in the Bowl app.

What we do

  • Use semantic HTML, headings and landmarks so screen readers can move through each page.
  • Give every image and chart a text description, and never use color alone to show meaning.
  • Keep text contrast at or above the WCAG AA ratios, in light and dark mode.
  • Make every link, button and form field usable with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Label every form field and explain errors in plain words.
  • Respect your device’s “reduce motion” setting: animations stop and show their final state.
  • Let text resize up to 200% without losing content.

Known limits

Some animated illustrations on the home page are decorative. Their content is also given in text next to them. Screenshots of the app show sample data; the same information is described in the page text.

Tell us about a problem

If something on our website or in the app is hard to use, please tell us. Email info@get-bowl.com with the page or screen and what happened. We aim to reply within 2 business days and to fix the problem or offer another way to get the information.

You can also write to us at Elis Group LLC dba Bowl, 355 NE 194th Ln, Miami, FL 33179.
