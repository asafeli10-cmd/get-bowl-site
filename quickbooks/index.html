QuickBooks Online

Bowl reads your QuickBooks. It never changes it.

Bowl connects to QuickBooks Online read only. It never writes, edits or deletes anything in your books.

Connect from the Bowl app

What Bowl reads

  • Accounts and vendors
  • Bills, open and paid
  • Expenses
  • The monthly Profit & Loss

Bowl reads the last 12 months when you connect, then checks for changes once a day.

What Bowl does with it

  • Rent, insurance, utilities and other costs Bowl has no scanned bill for come from QuickBooks.
  • Unpaid bills show up in Open invoices.
  • Nothing counts twice: when a QuickBooks entry matches an invoice or bill already in Bowl, Bowl keeps its own copy and ignores the QuickBooks one.
  • Food and supplies cost still come only from the invoices you scan.

Connect

  1. Open the Bowl app.
  2. Go to Settings → QuickBooks.
  3. Tap Connect QuickBooks and sign in with Intuit.

Does your accountant manage QuickBooks? From the same screen you can send them a one-time link, and they connect it for you. They don’t need a Bowl account.

Disconnect any time

In Bowl, go to Settings → QuickBooks → Disconnect. That revokes Bowl’s access at Intuit right away.
